This study investigates English as an instrument of soft power, examining its role as symbolic capital and as a vector of power in international relations.

Using a hypothetical-deductive approach and bibliographic and documentary research, the study analyzes how the diffusion of the English language contributes to the reinforcement of cultural and political hegemonies. The research also examines the role of new technologies, particularly large language models (LLMs), as potential instruments of digital persuasion and power modulation.

The findings indicate that the global diffusion of English extends beyond linguistic communication, functioning as a mechanism of strategic attraction and symbolic domination capable of influencing identities, values, and perceptions across different societies.

**Keywords:** Soft power; English language; geopolitical influence.
